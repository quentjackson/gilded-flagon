The Gilded Flagon

Three concoctions brewed for weary adventurers. Choose your potion, or let the d20 choose for you.

Greater Potion of Healing

Potion, rare

Components
Rose vodka, rosé wine, pomegranate juice, elderflower liqueur
Garnish
Pomegranate seeds
Hit Points
4d4 + 4 (morale only)

Restorative. When you drink this potion you regain the will to keep fighting. The red liquid glimmers when agitated and smells faintly of a temple garden after rain.

Potion of Shadow Blend

Potion, uncommon (requires a dimly lit corner)

Components
Spiced rum, cola, lime juice, cherry bitters
Duration
Until your glass is empty
Skills
Stealth +5, Bartering −1

Umbral Draught. While in dim light you can take the Hide action as a bonus action. The rogue who asked for “something dark” is already drinking it. A cherry stirs at the bottom like a watching eye.

The Evocation Spell

3rd-level evocation (ritual: last call)

Casting Time
1 action, shaken
Range
Self (20-foot radius of warmth)
Components
V, S, M (cinnamon whiskey, spiced rum, cranberry juice)
Damage
8d6 fire, to your throat

Bright Streak. A bright streak flashes from your glass and blossoms with a low roar into cinnamon flame. Make a Constitution saving throw. On a success you say “ooh” anyway. Warlocks report it pairs well with Eldritch Blast.
